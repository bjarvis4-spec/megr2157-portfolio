Task B — Product Analysis
Product - Residential Door Hinge
I chose a residential door hinge because I use my door hinge every day to go in and out of my appartment and it fairly simplistic. A hinge is a simple mechanical device, however it demonstrates how geometry can control motion and support a load.
Primary Function - The primary function of a door hinge is to support the weight of a door while allowing it to rotate about a fixed axis.
Governing Model - The primary mechanical task is rotational about a fixed axis. The hinge allows the door to rotate while restricting translation.
The weight of the door creates a moment about the hinge axis 
M=FD
(M) = moment
(F) = force, weight of the door
(d) = perpendicular distance from the hinge axis to the force
The hinge must support the door's weight while also allowing the door to rotate .
Assumption: The hinge and door can be treated as solid objects. This means small deformations will not affect the motion of the door.
